Tag palettes: original and proposed

Each drop is drawn from the same pixel art; only its colours change. Numbers are the median contrast of the drop's outline against the surface behind it. A Tag is a graphic, so it needs at least :1 (WCAG 1.4.11). See them move in the animation prototype (or with the original palette).

What changed and why

  • Wood: neon green → jade. The neon green nearly disappears on light backgrounds (outline :1 against the app's light panel). Jade, with shadows that lean towards teal, reads as leaves rather than a highlighter, and clears 3:1 on every surface (worst :1).
  • Metal: flat grey → cool silver. Flat grey reads as "disabled" and looks like the offline state. Silver keeps the outline as strong (worst :1) and gets a brighter shine.
  • Water, fire and soil are unchanged, as asked. Soil's yellow is also low on light backgrounds (:1); a darker soil outline would fix that without changing its colour, if wanted later.
  • The proposed palette is built in palettes.py from the water drop's own shading, so wood and metal keep exactly the same light and shadow as the other elements.
