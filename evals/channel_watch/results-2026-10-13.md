## What it shows

- **Decisions is nearly binary.** 34 of its 45 scores are ≤ 0.02 or ≥ 0.98. It
  separates this set perfectly, but a threshold gives little control, and its
  mistakes on harder real traffic are likely to be confident ones.
- **Jev is graded.** Its scores spread between 0.10 and 0.97.
  - At 0.9 it was precise but missed inclusive questions.
  - Its highest-scoring negatives were talk about Tag (praise 0.88,
    complaint 0.75), an aside after a finished exchange (0.78), and “tag the
    release” (0.81). The 0.9 margin is thin there.

## Limits

- 27 to 45 cases, labelled by the author against the same criteria the prompt
  states. This tests whether a classifier follows the criteria, not real
  traffic.
- It is not a basis for changing thresholds. The harness recommends one only
  after 200 or more labelled messages from a deployment; the 0.9 defaults stay.
